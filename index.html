Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you guarantee my account will be recovered?

No. Only TikTok can review an account and decide whether access or a feature can be restored. We provide guidance and do not guarantee a particular result.

Do I need to share my password or OTP?

No. Never share your password, OTP, passkey, backup code or recovery code with us or anyone else. You should complete sign-in and official verification yourself.

Will you submit an appeal for me?

We can help you understand the process and prepare the wording or information. You should review and submit the appeal through TikTok's official channels.

Are you an official TikTok service provider?

No. Zen Account Service is an independent assistance service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially connected to TikTok or TikTok Shop.

How much does the service cost?

Please contact us on WhatsApp to ask about the scope and any applicable service fee before deciding to proceed.

Important notice: Zen Account Service is an independent assistance service, not TikTok or TikTok Shop. We do not sell, transfer, rent or trade accounts; bypass platform restrictions; use fake transactions; or promise account recovery. All account reviews and restoration decisions are made by TikTok.
